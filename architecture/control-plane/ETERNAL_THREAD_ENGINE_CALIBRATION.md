## Originating intent
The Eternal Thread Engine was established as a continuity discipline: preserve architectural memory, maintain distinctions across time, inspect before acting, keep human decision sovereign, and prevent novelty or local convenience from silently rewriting the system's governing boundaries.

The provenance arc remains aligned with that intent. It repeatedly chose inspection over implementation, falsification over affirmation, and explicit uncertainty over invented closure.

## What the arc preserved
1. **Human sovereignty:** no candidate self-selected, authorized itself, or entered implementation without a human gate.
2. **Continuity:** each artifact inherits explicit distinctions and unresolved state from the previous one.
3. **Inspectability:** claims are separated from evidence, and repository grounding is distinguished from semantic possibility.
4. **Boundary discipline:** PassSpec, K15 and K3 were not modified while the provenance problem was being studied.
5. **Clarity over intensity:** failed candidates remain useful because each failure closes a false solution class.
6. **No autonomous escalation:** the Control Plane remains a contract/observation layer, not a new execution authority.

## What evolved beyond the initial trajectory
The arc became more epistemic than the initial architecture map anticipated. It did not merely map components and seams; it discovered a precise distinction between:

`CAUSAL CLAIM → CAUSAL FACT → EVIDENCE OF CAUSAL FACT`

The third remains uninstantiated. This is an earned frontier, not a defect to be hidden.

## Five architectural dialects now visible
1. **Lab:** staged observation, calibration, possibility, human decision and governed binding.
2. **Control Plane:** contract, observation, drift, exception and boundary semantics.
3. **Provenance:** evidence, origin, authority-event semantics and collision analysis.
4. **Spiral Grammar:** symbolic transformation vocabulary used as an interface/framework, not an authority source.
5. **Oracle:** structured encounter with possibility; pattern generation does not become truth or decision authority.

## Shared meta-grammar
Across the dialects, a useful common structure is:

`STATE → OBSERVATION → EVALUATION → DECISION → NEXT STATE`

Transformation connects states and provenance cross-cuts the chain. This is a semantic meta-grammar, not yet a claim that Spiral Grammar is the foundational implementation language of the Control Plane.

## Calibration verdict
**ALIGNED.** The provenance arc serves the originating continuity intent and has strengthened it by making epistemic boundaries explicit.
