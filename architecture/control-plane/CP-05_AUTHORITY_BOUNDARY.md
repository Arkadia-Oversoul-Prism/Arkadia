# CP-05 — AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

**Status:** persisted / schema-only / constitutional boundary

CP-05 establishes the authority chain used by the later provenance work:

`CAPABILITY → ELIGIBILITY → AUTHORIZATION → EXECUTION → VERIFICATION → PUBLICATION`

## Core invariants
- Capability does not imply authorization.
- Human approval is explicit, not inferred from capability existence.
- Execution cannot occur without the applicable authorization state.
- Publication and deployment remain distinct from mutation.
- Governance preparation does not itself grant Weaver mutation authority.
- The Lab remains bounded below the governed engineering mutation boundary.

## Role of CP-05 in the later arc
CP-05 supplied the authority boundary that CP-10 subsequently stress-tested from the provenance side. The provenance arc does not replace this chain. It asks what evidence is required before an authority determination can legitimately enter it.

## Non-goals
CP-05 does not define human-origin proof, select a provenance mechanism, or authorize autonomous execution.
