# Eden Food Systems — SolSpire Enterprise Pilot

Status: ARCHITECTURE SCAFFOLD / WEEK 1

Eden Food Systems is the first enterprise workload to be modelled against the existing SolSpire Enterprise layer.

This is not a separate intelligence system. It is an enterprise operating context projected through the canonical SolSpire workspace, WorkEvent spine, Workload model, project resources, and governed dashboard surfaces.

## Pilot

- Budget: ₦1,000,000
- Operating window: 4 weeks
- Objective: execute one controlled commodity transaction while building the minimum repeatable operating system.
- Motto: From Source to Market.
- Initial operating departments:
  1. Procurement & Commodity Operations
  2. Logistics & Distribution
  3. Packaging, Quality & Handling
  4. Marketing & Customer Acquisition
  5. Brand, Content & Customer Experience
  6. Digital Operations & Communications
  7. Operations Management, Customer Care & Finance
  8. Contingency / Reserve

## Architectural rule

The eight budget categories are resource streams. The eight departments are accountable desks. The eight execution phases are the transaction lifecycle. The dashboard is the projection that makes those three dimensions inspectable together.

## Truth boundary

The dashboard MUST distinguish:

- RECORDED — supported by a stored work event or artifact.
- COMMITTED — explicitly approved/assigned but not yet completed.
- ESTIMATED — planning assumption.
- UNKNOWN — not yet supplied or evidenced.

The dashboard must never manufacture financial performance, customer status, inventory, delivery status, or authority from missing data.

## Authority boundary

The existing repository binds SolSpire workspace ownership to the verified authenticated Firebase subject. The phrase "Sovereign Architect ID" can be represented as an identity/owner reference in enterprise metadata, but no secret identifier, payment tier, or elevated authority should be hard-coded into this enterprise package.

Governance remains governed by the existing Arkadia permissions model. The enterprise product is an operational workspace, not a new governance authority.
