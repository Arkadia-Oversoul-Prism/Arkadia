# Eden Food Systems — Week 1 / Monday Control Sheet

Objective: establish the operating machine before transaction execution.

## 08:30 — Chief of Operations stand-up

Required outputs:

- Pilot objective confirmed.
- ₦1,000,000 budget confirmed.
- Eight departments instantiated.
- One accountable owner assigned to every department.
- Four-week operating clock opened.
- Dashboard owner assigned.
- Blocker register opened.

No task may remain ownerless.

## 09:00 — Procurement

Tasks:

- Review R01/R02 market intelligence already produced.
- Reconfirm current commodity candidates.
- Record source, indicative buy price, destination, indicative sell price, logistics estimate and quality constraints.
- Produce a shortlist of transaction candidates.

Required output: Commodity Candidate Register.

Truth status:
- Estimates remain ESTIMATED.
- Any current supplier quotation must carry evidence reference and timestamp.

## 10:00 — Marketing & Customer Acquisition

Tasks:

- Define initial customer segments.
- Separate destination buyers from general consumer demand.
- Identify first reachable buyer pools.
- Draft the first demand-generation hypothesis.
- Define the first measurable marketing test.

Required output: Customer Segment + Demand Test Brief.

## 11:00 — Digital Operations

Tasks:

- Resolve canonical SolSpire workspace.
- Create/confirm Eden enterprise workspace.
- Create the pilot project/workload reference.
- Create the shared document/data surface.
- Define the dashboard data contract.
- Establish the record naming convention.

Required output: Eden Enterprise Workspace Ready.

## 12:30 — Midday control check

Only exceptions:

- missing owner
- missing evidence
- budget ambiguity
- unavailable source/buyer intelligence
- digital access failure
- governance/authority ambiguity

## 14:00 — Operations / Finance

Tasks:

- Load ₦1m budget allocation.
- Establish committed/spent/remaining fields.
- Define approval gates.
- Establish expense evidence requirements.
- Create the reconciliation ledger structure.

Required output: Pilot Finance Control Sheet.

## 15:00 — Brand / Customer Experience

Tasks:

- Lock the business identity already agreed: Eden Food Systems.
- Lock motto: From Source to Market.
- Prepare the canonical brand brief.
- Define customer promise and initial offer presentation.
- Prepare asset checklist: logo, bio, product template, WhatsApp catalogue, FAQ, social templates.

Required output: Brand Activation Brief.

## 16:00 — Logistics + Quality

Tasks:

- Map likely routes.
- Establish transport quotation fields.
- Define commodity quality/packaging checklist.
- Identify receiving-point requirements.

Required output: Logistics + QC Readiness Sheet.

## 17:30 — Daily close

The Chief of Operations records:

- tasks completed
- tasks blocked
- decisions made
- money committed/spent
- new suppliers
- new buyers
- marketing actions
- assets created
- operational risks

## 19:00 — Dashboard close

The dashboard must answer, without interpretation:

1. What are we trying to execute?
2. Who owns each workstream?
3. What has been evidenced?
4. What is estimated?
5. What is committed?
6. What is spent?
7. What is blocked?
8. What decision is next?
9. What must happen tomorrow?

## Definition of Done for Monday

- Enterprise workspace exists.
- Pilot workload exists.
- Eight departments exist.
- Accountable owners exist.
- ₦1m budget is mapped.
- Commodity shortlist exists.
- Customer segment brief exists.
- Digital operating surface exists.
- Brand activation brief exists.
- Finance control exists.
